## Content

### LinkedIn

Tomorrow, the Verana Foundation speaks at the Eclipse Foundation's Models4Privacy Interest Group webinar: Why AI Agents must have their own identity?

AI agents are starting to transact, negotiate, and act on our behalf. Before each interaction, three questions need verifiable answers: Who is this agent? Who operates it? What is it allowed to do?

We will look at why answering them through a central registry creates a privacy trap (every interaction becomes linkable, detectable, and disclosable to a central observer), and how a decentralized public trust layer answers them instead, with verifiable credentials and cryptographic trust registries. Ariel Gentile (CTO, 2060) joins with a live demo on the Verana testnet, and we will close with an open discussion on privacy engineering for decentralized trust: threat modeling the spec, privacy vocabulary alignment, and conformance testing.

If you work on privacy engineering, digital identity, or agentic AI, join us. Free and open to everyone.
