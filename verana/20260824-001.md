### LinkedIn

Verifiable credentials are becoming a reality. Issuer, holder, verifier: the trust triangle is standardized, and implementations are arriving in every sector.

The next problem is interconnection. Four challenges: create new ecosystems at will and monetize them. Scale across borders. Let services and AI agents authenticate mutually. And discover trusted services, anywhere.

Our new 3-minute video shows how Verana answers all four: sovereign ecosystems, verifiable services, and one public Trust Graph. Open, neutral, cross-border, owned by no one.

Create your trust registry, publish your governance framework, credential schemas and business models, and onboard your participants. Deploy your services and AI agents, attach their credentials, and you are visible in the Trust Graph: any person, service, or AI agent can find you, mutually authenticate with verifiable credentials, and connect. Trust, established before the first message.

Behind it all: open specifications on open standards, open-source software, and a non-profit foundation managed by its community. Free to use, free to join.
