# Vesta Appliances: from impostors to Proof-of-Trust, now in the FIDES Use Case Catalog

## Platform

## Content

### LinkedIn

A repair technician rings your doorbell. The badge looks right. The uniform looks right. Nothing about either can be checked.

The Vesta use case, now published in the FIDES Use Case Catalog, is the Verana Playground's answer to that moment. Vesta Appliances (demo) is a fictional Swiss manufacturer that runs an authorized repair network: subsidiaries accredit repair companies, repair companies issue technician badges, and the customer's wallet verifies the whole chain at the door, in one scan.

The impostor in the story is the point. Umbra Repairs (demo) is a real, verifiable company with a perfect uniform, and its badge still fails, because verifiable is not the same as authorized.

Everything runs live on the Verana testnet: one open-source business wallet (VS Agent) per participant, real registries, real credentials. And the same demo runs on every open-source personal wallet integrated in the playground. One trust layer, many wallets.
