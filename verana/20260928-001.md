### LinkedIn (personal)

I was at the UK Parliament for the Better Hiring Institute's event on the future of hiring, where David Rennie, Chief Trust Officer at Orchestrating Identity, introduced the room to Trust Networks.

His starting point: hiring still runs on documents nobody can verify. Candidates email passport scans to strangers, employers re-check the same facts for weeks, and fake job ads harvest identity documents.

In a Trust Network, the checks go both ways. Employers prove they are legitimate before a candidate shares anything, and qualifications, employment history and right to work arrive as verifiable credentials, checked against a public registry at the moment of asking. A fake recruiter fails because it cannot prove it belongs.

Want to feel it? Orchestrating Identity authored a hands-on use case on the Verana playground: create an applicant, collect credentials in your wallet, apply to two similar job ads, and watch your wallet refuse the wrong one: https://playground.testnet.verana.network/usecases/bhi

Thank you David, and the Better Hiring Institute, for putting verifiable trust on the parliamentary agenda.
