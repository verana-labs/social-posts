### LinkedIn

I'm speaking at the Global Digital Collaboration Conference this Thursday.

Session: Decentralized Trust Mechanisms for Serving Local and Global Needs
Day 3 · 09:00 to 09:50 · Room K · Palexpo Geneva

As digital interactions span organizations, sectors, and borders, centralized trust models hit their limits: scalability, privacy, interoperability, resilience. We will explore how trust can be established, managed, and verified across ecosystems without a single central authority: verifiable credentials, cryptographic trust registries, did:webvh, DeDi, OpenID Federation, Public Key Directories, and KERI, and how these mechanisms can serve Digital Public Infrastructure and cross-border collaboration.

I will bring the Verana perspective: public trust registries as shared, sovereign infrastructure, where local trust requirements and global interoperability coexist through open standards.

Honored to share the stage with Ciaran Carolan (ICAO), Drummond Reed (First Person Cooperative), Scott Casey (Networks for Humanity), Pierre-Antoine Champin (W3C / Inria), Michael Jones (Self-Issued Consulting), Lucy Yang, Daniela Barbosa (Linux Foundation Decentralized Trust), and Clare Rowley (GLEIF). Co-organized by UNESCO and the DPI Group (Aapti Institute, Interledger Foundation, MOSIP, Networks for Humanity).

See you Thursday at 09:00 in Room K.
