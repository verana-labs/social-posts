The Verana Foundation welcomes [INTEXUS](https://intexus.la/) as an Associate member.

If you work in digital identity in Latin America, you know INTEXUS. From Colombia, they issue physical and digital identification for governments and enterprises across the region, operate secure payment and card issuance infrastructure, and serve more than 4,000 clients, including banks, airlines, and public institutions.

> "QUOTE PENDING DAVID ROJAS APPROVAL. Proposed: Our clients ask for digital identity they can trust and that no single vendor controls. Joining the Verana Foundation lets us build that on open, sovereign infrastructure, together with the whole ecosystem."
>
> David Rojas, [TITLE TBC], INTEXUS

**They built first, and joined after.** The [INTEXUS Wallet](https://playground.testnet.verana.network) already runs on the Verana playground, and in September INTEXUS organized Creando un mundo confiable, a demonstration tour across Costa Rica, Guatemala, and Panama showing verifiable credentials and the open trust layer live to governments and enterprises of the region.

That is exactly the pattern a public trust layer needs: the companies that already operate identity infrastructure choosing open, sovereign foundations over proprietary silos. Associate members do more than support the Foundation: they fund and govern an open trust layer that no single vendor owns.
