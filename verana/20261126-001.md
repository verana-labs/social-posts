### LinkedIn (personal)

I do not recommend OVHcloud Managed Kubernetes for production. Here is why, with facts.

We upgraded our Kubernetes clusters from the OVHcloud control panel, to a version OVHcloud itself offers. Since that upgrade, pods can no longer mount their persistent disks. Databases, indexers, every stateful service on three clusters: down or one restart away from it. That was more than 20 hours ago.

These are development clusters. That is the good news, and the only reason I am writing a post instead of an incident report to customers.

Why this is not a problem we can solve ourselves:

1. The upgrade is a button in their console. Nothing was hand-rolled.
2. Everything we can observe points to the storage driver that OVHcloud runs on the nodes. In a managed service we have no access to it. We cannot restart it, patch it or roll it back.
3. A brand new node shows the same failure. There is no workaround on the customer side.

So the only lever left is support. We are on OVHcloud's Premium Advanced support plan, a plan we pay for precisely to get an answer when something like this happens. Eight hours ago we opened a ticket with the exact error, the evidence, and a reproduction on a second, independent cluster. Two follow-ups later, one of them saying "100% service downtime", we have not received a single human reply. As I write this, nothing on their status page mentions the problem either.

That is the deal with a managed service: you give up control, and in exchange someone answers when the managed part breaks. If nobody answers, you get the worst of both worlds: no control and no help.

Every provider ships a bad upgrade one day. I do not judge a provider on the incident. I judge it on the first hours after it. On that measure, I cannot put production workloads here, and I would not advise you to.

OVHcloud, the ticket is CS17240957. I will gladly update this post with good news.
