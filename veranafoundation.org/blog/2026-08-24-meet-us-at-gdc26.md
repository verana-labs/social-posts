Meet us at the [Global Digital Collaboration Conference](https://globaldigitalcollaboration.org/), September 1 to 3 at Palexpo Geneva.

GDC brings together thousands of builders, standards bodies, and policymakers across digital identity: wallets, verifiable credentials, trust frameworks, technical standards, and regulation. Exactly the conversations the internet's missing trust layer needs.

We will be there to talk Verifiable Trust: public trust registries, sovereign ecosystems, and how services and AI agents identify and trust each other before the first message. If you are building wallets, trust frameworks, or agentic AI, come find us.

- 📍 Palexpo Geneva, September 1 to 3
